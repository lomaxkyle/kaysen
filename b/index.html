About

Kaysen Lomax is a photographer and multimedia creator in Jonesboro, Arkansas. She has photographed seniors, couples, families, and events across Northeast Arkansas since 2018, and since January 2026 has shot weddings as a contract photographer with Rustic Pine Media.

She also shoots and edits video and designs posters, invitations, and ads, and spent a year as Marketing Coordinator for Southern Pharmacy running its social media, photographing its events, and designing the graphics that went with them. Her work placed first in Portrait Photography and second in Sports Photography at the Great A-State Media Fest.

She graduates from Arkansas State University in December 2026 with a B.S. in Mass Communications. What she cares about is simple: a fresh idea, a straightforward message, and a finished piece people remember.
