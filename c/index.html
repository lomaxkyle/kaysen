Project · Marketing

Southern Pharmacy

Role
Marketing Coordinator
When
Aug 2024 – May 2025, with pieces through 2026
Scope
Social media · print · events · campaign pitch · website concept
Where
Jonesboro, Arkansas

Southern Pharmacy is a hometown pharmacy with stores across Northeast Arkansas. For ten months I was its Marketing Coordinator: I ran the social accounts, photographed products and events, and designed everything that went out in-store and online. The work kept coming after the job ended.

The biggest piece was a full campaign pitch under the line "Big Time Service. Small Town Care." It ran 43 slides: audience and tone, 40 scheduled social posts, 10 radio spots, 5 video concepts, a website concept, and a four-phase rollout plan.

Around it: a weekly Wellness Tip Wednesday series, launches for MPowder and CarePacks, the Hormone House Party invitation that brought 50 of the region's most influential women to a menopause awareness event, a tornado response post the night two towns were hit, and in December 2025 the announcement that two locations were passing to another pharmacy.
